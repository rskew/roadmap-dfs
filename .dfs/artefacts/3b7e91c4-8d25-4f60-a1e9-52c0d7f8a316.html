Do the arrow keys step or scroll?

On the task list and on a task, the web page now makes Up and Down step to the previous or next row, as j and k do, instead of scrolling the page. Pick a mode, click the pane and press ↓ or ↑ to feel the difference.

  • Step: matches the terminal screen, where arrows and j/k move the same cursor; a long page cannot be scrolled by the arrows from a row (PageUp, PageDown, Space and the wheel still scroll).
  • Scroll: keeps the browser's habit; only j/k move between rows, so the arrows and j/k do different things.
