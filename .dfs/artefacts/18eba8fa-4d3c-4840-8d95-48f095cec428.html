    What would show it wrong

    A sandboxed page makes any write the two routes above do not allow (answers a raise that does not name it, or gets through to /api), or the author wants no page, even the one the raise names, to answer without them pressing Record.

    The evidence so far

    In a real browser, the page in the answer form posts to its route and the raise is answered; its JSON post to /api/walk/start is blocked at the preflight. The server refuses /api/answer with origin null (403) whether the body is JSON or text, an answer from a page the raise does not name (409), and a post from another website's origin (403).
