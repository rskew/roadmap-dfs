A draft only leaves the walk's choice

A task marked draft (a draft entry in its own log, undone by ready) is never the one the walk picks, and holds no fence. It stays an open task: pinning it with n, or running it by hand, works it.

Try it: mark tasks draft, pin one, see what the walk takes

    What would show it wrong

    You want a draft to hold the tasks nested under it, or a fence after it, until it is ready; or you want the walk to keep working a draft you pinned after that one chain ends.
