Each row prints the luminance contrast of its wash against the page, and of the muted text on the wash. Dark washes went from 1.14–1.17 to 1.27–1.39; muted text is lifted so it stays above 5:1. These are calculated figures, and the panels use the same hex values as the page. Your screen is the real test: if the right-hand panel is still too faint, say so and the washes go up at some cost to muted-text contrast.
