A thin line where the bottom buttons end

On a phone the bars at the foot of the page (the tabs, the node's Prev/Next/Flagged/Back, the chat's bar) run straight into the strip Android keeps for its gesture handle, with nothing to show where the button stops. A thumb aiming for Back lands on the handle.

What is built

A 1px ink line on the lower edge of the buttons in each bottom bar, above any inset the phone reports for its gesture strip, so it marks where the button ends. Not drawn on a screen wider than 900px, where those bars sit at the top. Real page, Pixel 7 size, node bar:

What would show it wrong

You still hit the gesture handle when you reach for Back with the line there: then the buttons need lifting off the edge, a 10–12px blank strip under them on every view, which costs screen height but puts a dead zone between Back and the handle.
