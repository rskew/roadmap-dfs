Why archive sometimes opens the item

Two causes could make a tap on a row's archive icon open the row. Both are drawn to scale below, from the real page at 360px (the W4 row: 65px tall, the archive box 51 by 32px, the icon 18px inside it). Cause 1: the finger lands beside the icon. Cause 2: the list moves between the press and the release.

Cause 1: the finger lands beside the icon

Drag the finger up or down. Measured in headless Chromium with real touch taps at 2px steps: before the 44px change only the box answered archive; every point outside it opened the row.

  • the row: a press here opens the item
  • the 44px band, now (dashed)
  • the 51x32 box, before
  • the 18px icon
  • the finger

Cause 2: the list moves between press and release

A real write made while the finger rests on the W2 icon, then the release. Pick what the project wrote. Shifts are the measured movement of the icon at 360px; clicks are what the page received.

  • W1's row, above (yellow: the part it grows by)
  • W2's row, with its icon box (green)
  • the finger: it does not move

The guard already makes cause 2 harmless. The question is which cause the author's "sometimes" is, since that decides where further work goes: a larger target for cause 1, nothing more for cause 2.

What would show it wrong

The "sometimes" is mostly cause 1. Refute it if archive opens the item while the list is plainly still and your finger began on the icon itself, or if your project writes row-changing events far more often than this roadmap's history (several a minute).
