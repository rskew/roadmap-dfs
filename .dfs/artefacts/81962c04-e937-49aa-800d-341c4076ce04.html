What the chat's ↑ button goes to

The Goal says "scroll to the top of current message" and does not say which message. The work takes it to be the one being read, and stops a busy agent pulling a scrolled-up reader back to the end. Move the reader and see where ↑ goes.

What would show it wrong

The author means a different message, for example the one the agent is answering or the last agent answer, or wants a reader who scrolled up carried to the end when the agent finishes.

The evidence

The chat_top browser flow passes: from the end ↑ lands on the nearest message starting above the view, a further press steps back, and a busy turn's redraws leave the position alone. Without the change to chatDraw the same flow fails (3210 != 74).
