What the keep rule asks of replays shown the answer

Three of four W55 replays were shown the original’s conclusions, so at that rate (exposure 0.75) 24 replays leave about 3 unexposed replays an arm. Choose the exposure to see how often each rule keeps a change (100,000 simulated runs, scripts/chance.py).

The choice

Chosen: keep on a lead of 4 of 12 over all replays, and also require at least two unexposed replays an arm whose pass fraction for the changed arm is not below the control’s. Not chosen: a margin on unexposed replays alone (impossible at 3 an arm: a lead cannot exceed the arm), or no loop until a box hides the answer (nothing is learned meanwhile; the same rule applies then, with every replay unexposed).

Wrong if the exposure is much lower than 0.75 (then a scaled margin on the unexposed replays can be asked), or you would rather no candidate run until a box hides the answer.
