Replay keep rule: how often chance keeps a change that does nothing

A change is tested on 4 tasks, each replayed 3 times under the control and 3 times under the change (12 first-review outcomes per arm, pass or fail). It is kept if it passes at least M more of the 12 than the control, and no task passes more than 1 fewer of its 3.

The model

Each replay passes its first review with probability p, independently of the others; the two arms have p0 (control) and p1 (change). Per task the passes out of 3 are binomial; the total over 4 tasks is compared. The numbers are exact sums over every outcome, not simulations. Replays of one task are likely more alike than independent ones, which would widen the noise.
