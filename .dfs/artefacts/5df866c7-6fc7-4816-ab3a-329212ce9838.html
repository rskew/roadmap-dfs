What would show it wrong

Wrong if, in the installed app, tapping an artefact link already opens a window or sheet with its own close or back control. Then the viewer adds nothing and the link stays as it is.

The evidence so far

Read in the code: the page links artefacts with target="_blank" to /artefacts/<name>, inside the manifest's scope / with display: standalone; the server sends that file with a sandbox CSP and no page of its own around it. Not tried on a real phone: that is what you can tell me.
