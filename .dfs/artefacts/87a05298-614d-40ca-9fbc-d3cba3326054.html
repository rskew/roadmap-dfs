W75: sessions in a worktree read the law as the main checkout has it now

A session works in .dfs/worktrees/<task>, whose copy of .dfs/ROADMAP.md is as it was when the branch was cut.

You edit .dfs/ROADMAP.md in your checkout (committed or not)
↓
The next session's briefing reads main's file
↓
The session follows the new law line at once
You edit .dfs/ROADMAP.md in your checkout
↓
The branch keeps its older copy until it is rebased
↓
The session works under the old law; the new line only reaches it at the merge's rebase

Today: paths.law() finds the main checkout by git's common directory and the briefing, critic brief and review brief all carry its current text.

The alternative: rebase the branch onto main at every run, which brings all of main's code in mid-task and can conflict there.

Wrong if you leave a half-written ROADMAP.md edit uncommitted and did not mean running sessions to follow it, or you want the law frozen at the branch's cut.
