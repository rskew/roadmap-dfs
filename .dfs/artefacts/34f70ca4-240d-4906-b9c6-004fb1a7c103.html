What "steering" a chat means

The page's chat runs one headless agent turn per message. While it answers, Send is off and Chats.send refuses. The author sends "use the other file" two steps in. Pick what happens to it.

    Steer reuses the inbox hooks a chain session already has (inbox.py): the message is in the agent's next request, and one that arrives after its last step becomes one more turn. It needs those hooks to fire in a headless chat turn for each of claude, codex, opencode and kiro.
