Other choices made in the build

  • Up on a non-empty draft steps only with the caret at position 0 on the first line, so a multi-line draft keeps Up for moving the caret.
  • Two identical messages in a row show once.
  • Stepping is per open chat and is not kept after a reload. The unsent draft is kept as before.
  • The terminal chat hands the keyboard to the agent's own program, so its Up and Down are that program's; it was not changed.
