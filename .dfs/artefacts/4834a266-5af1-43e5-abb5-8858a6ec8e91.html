Does the name field lose your caret?

The assumption: on a phone, a one-line text box scrolls its text sideways and does not keep the caret in view once the name is longer than the box, and a box that wraps the name has nothing to scroll sideways. Try both below, on your phone, with the long name already in them.

The field to try

What to do

Tap in the field, move the caret by dragging it or by holding the space bar, add words at the end, then at the start. Watch whether the text keeps the caret on screen and whether the caret sits where the letters are. Switch field kind and spacing and compare.

What would show it wrong

The one-line field keeps the caret in view on your phone, or the wrapped field loses it as well. Then the fault is elsewhere (the dialog's own scrolling under the keyboard) and the fix is not this.

The evidence so far

Chromium's iPhone 13 emulation follows the caret in the one-line field (End scrolled 0 to 517 of 823 px, Home back to 0), so the fault is not in Blink. The wrapped field has no horizontal scroll to lose (scrollWidth equal to clientWidth after 92 characters). Neither says anything about a real phone's input, which only you can try here.
