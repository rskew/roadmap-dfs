Which "manually confirmed" items lose the tick?

The author can overrule a node's verdict by writing a correction. The tree then treats the node as confirmed, and draws a tick, though no session built it. Pick a reading of the goal to see which rows lose the tick.

The reading

Rule on this node: does the work take "item" to mean a node?

What would show it wrong

If "item" means a whole task in the list, an accepted task is the case meant, and the tick belongs off the task row instead of, or as well as, the node.

The evidence so far

An accepted task has every live node confirmed by a session first (it cannot be accepted otherwise), so its tick is true. Only a node confirmed by the author's correction can be ticked with nothing built: it may be open, started, parked or refuted in the file.
