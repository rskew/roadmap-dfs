What hangs when a chat hits a usage limit

A page chat on the screen is an interactive claude. When the account hits its limit, claude opens a menu and waits at it. Switch the view to see what a message does.

claude answers "You've hit your session limit · resets 5:20pm (UTC)" and opens /rate-limit-options: "What do you want to do?" (seen in the real chat log of W68).
↓ the page sends a message
The relay pastes the text and presses Enter into the menu. The menu is not a prompt, so claude records nothing.
↓
The page shows the message as sent, nobody is answering, and no limit line is shown. It hangs, out of step with the agent.
The relay sees the menu on the agent's screen and reads the reset time from it.
↓
The chat state carries the usage limit with its reset time, so the page shows the limit line at once.
↓ the page sends a message
The relay presses Esc (the menu says "Esc to cancel"), then types the message. claude submits it, answers that the limit is hit, and records both. The page and the agent agree.
