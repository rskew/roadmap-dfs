What would show it wrong

An author writes a page that passes and gives the reader nothing to change (the decorative button), or a page the reader can operate that is refused (CSS tabs, controls built by script). Both are possible here, so the test is a floor, not a proof: the screenshot and the author are what judge whether the controls say anything.
