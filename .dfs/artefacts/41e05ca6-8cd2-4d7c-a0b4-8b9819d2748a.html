Does a fine pointer mean "has a keyboard"?

The web chat sends on Enter only when matchMedia("(pointer: fine)") is true. Elsewhere Enter adds a line and Ctrl/cmd+Enter sends. Pick a device to see what Enter does under the rule now, and under each alternative.

RuleWhat it does on this device
Now: Enter sends if pointer: fine
Enter sends if any-pointer: fine
Enter always sends

Hypothesis: the primary pointer says whether a physical keyboard is in use. It is wrong for a tablet or touch laptop with a keyboard whose primary pointer reports coarse: Enter adds a line there, and only Ctrl/cmd+Enter sends.

Seen in headless chromium (profiles, not real hardware): desktop reports fine, iPhone 13 and iPad Pro 11 report coarse. A real keyboard-case tablet was not available to test.
