The 71% in the raise was lines, not bytes: a session line is about 60 bytes, a raise or review entry is thousands.

  • cut_off: a session line with no end after it is a session that was killed. One line written at the close would leave no trace of a kill.
  • last_session, _since_last: when the running session began; the critic cadence counts session lines.
  • note_sessions: groups a review's or critic's notes under the session line that opened them.
  • unacted_answers, review_due, accepted: the end line's time and outcome.

Merging them means a new in-progress marker (a file or a field) and rewriting seven readers to save at most 5.7% of the bytes.
