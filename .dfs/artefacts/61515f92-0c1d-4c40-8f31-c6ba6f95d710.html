The icon’s marks stay: a square root, amber forks, red bars where a branch stops, blue discs where one arrives.

What it shows now (seed 3 of the fifteen tried): a trunk from a root square at the foot, splitting two or three ways at angles off any grid, each branch about three quarters as long and 70% as wide as its parent, eight forks deep.
