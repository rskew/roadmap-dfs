Which window does a kiro session have?

Kiro sends a percentage of the window in use, not the window. The runner turns the percentage into tokens with a window it estimates from the breakdown (tokens / percent per part) and snaps to 128k, 200k, 1M or 2M. Tap a rule to see what it reads on the 3 real runs where the parts disagree (a 7.6% reading; the true window is the 1M of the other 51).

Evidence

54 runs with a breakdown, last 200 run logs

51 imply about 1M from every part (985,333 from tools: 5,912 tokens at 0.6%). 3 (rg6oahr3 run-1 and run-3, y2otka9c run-8) also carry a late kiroResponses part of 14,476 to 29,999 tokens at 4 to 7%, which implies about 360k to 430k. The median of the three estimates chose 200k there, so the 7.6% reading was 15k tokens, not 76k.
