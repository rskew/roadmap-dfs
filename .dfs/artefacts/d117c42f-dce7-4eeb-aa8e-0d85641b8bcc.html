What the archive fix covers

Two changes make a tap on archive archive: a 44px touch target, and a guard that runs the button the press began on. Pick a case to see what each does, and where each stops.

What is assumed

  • The "sometimes" is mostly a finger landing beside the icon, and 44 CSS px high (about 7mm) is enough to catch it. The 9.2mm one-handed-thumb figure would be about 58px. Nothing was measured on a phone.
  • When the list moves between press and release, the pressed button should win over the one the click landed on.
  • Only pointer clicks (detail above 0) are corrected, and buttons are told apart by data-act and data-id alone.
  • A press whose click lands on no button is dropped: the tap does nothing, and the item does not open.

What would show it wrong

You find 44px too small and want about 58px. Or you want a tap whose click lands on no button to archive too. Or you want the guard to tell buttons apart by more than act and id. Refute, and the work goes to that.
