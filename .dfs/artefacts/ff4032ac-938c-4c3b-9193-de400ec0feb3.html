Which hidden roadmap files the tool reports

Each card is one way a project's ignore rules can meet .dfs: what git then does with the roadmap, and what the new probe (hidden(), behind init and the warnings) says. Switch to see what would change if the assumption is wrong.

The assumption

A rule naming .dfs itself, with nothing under it tracked, is the supported choice to keep the roadmap as planning; every other way a roadmap part is ignored is a misconfiguration. Only the roadmap's parts are asked about (ROADMAP.md, order.md, items/, archive/, artefacts/, title, theme), so a cache in .dfs is not. Wrong if you keep .dfs as planning through a broader rule such as .* and want no warning, or want untracked files git would add reported too.
