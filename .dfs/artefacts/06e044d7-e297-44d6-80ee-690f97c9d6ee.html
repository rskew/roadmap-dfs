Which clock is "5:59 PM" on?

Codex ends a usage limit with "try again at 5:59 PM." It names no zone. The walk turns that into an instant to hold until. It assumes the time is on the clock of the machine the walk runs on, which is the machine codex ran on.

If the two clocks agree, or if they do not

Rule on this node: whether the walk may read codex's bare time in its own clock.

This page is also the answer to a raise. Choose above, then send it.

What would show it wrong

A codex run that stamps its "try again at" in a zone other than the walk's: the walk would hold too short (and be limited again) or too long (a day lost).
