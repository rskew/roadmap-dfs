What can pin the chat log to its end while Top scrolls?

Top starts a smooth scroll. If any code sets scrollTop = scrollHeight while the log is near its end, the scroll is cancelled and the first press seems to do nothing. The node assumes the keyboard-close resize (and a poll redraw) are the causes, and that holding the pin until scrollend cures them. Pick a view:

Tap ↑ (chatTop)starts scrollTo smooth; the hold TOP_SCROLL.on is set (not before the fix)
↓ keyboard closes, a poll arrives
fit() on visualViewport resize, scroll, and 100/300/600ms after focus changespins when within 40px of the end
chatDraw() on a poll that changes the logpins when within 80px of the end
chatGrow() on a window resizepins when within 4px of the end. Found while testing this node.
