What does a folded row count?

A folded node hides the nodes under it, so its row shows chips: open raises, unfinished nodes and assumptions below. The Goal does not say which nodes are "incomplete" or which count as "assumptions". The sample tree below is folded at its top. Switch the two readings and watch the chips move.

The two readings

Both off is what was built. A refuted or pruned node is never counted either way.

What would show this wrong

Wrong if you want a parked node counted as unfinished, or want an assumption you already confirmed or refuted to stay in the count. Refute this if the chips on a folded row should say something other than what the first switch and the second switch show when both are off.

Evidence so far: over the 524 rows with children in this repo's task trees, 42 have a parked node below that the build leaves out, and 3 have a ruled-on assumption it leaves out. Only 1 row has an unfinished node below, and 163 show an assumption, because a Hypothesis counts until the author rules on it.
