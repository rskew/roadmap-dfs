Keep a change by comparing it with the control, pair by pair

The first-review pass count cannot keep a change when the control already passes 9 of 12. The proposal: one pair is one task replayed twice in the same sitting, once under the control and once under the change, and each pair is judged better, same or worse.

One pair: the same task, control then change, same sitting. Count the reviews that said issues, and the credits to a finished, reviewed task.
↓ judged
Better if the change had fewer issues reviews, or the same number and at most 70% of the control's credits (worse: more issues reviews, or the same and at least 1/0.7 = 1.43 times the credits; otherwise same). A pair with an unpriced session is run again.
↓ three pairs, on W5, W36 and W22
3 of 3 better: keep.
↓ two better and one same (anything else stops here: 4 of 5 can no longer happen)
Two more pairs, on W11 and W5. 4 of 5 better and none worse: keep.
↓ otherwise
Reject. Recorded as "not shown", never "shown useless".
