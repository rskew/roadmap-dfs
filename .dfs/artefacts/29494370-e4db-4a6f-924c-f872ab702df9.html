Reorder rows are tall

In Reorder mode each task row holds its id, a goal that wraps, a line of status tags, a reason line, a strip of four 44px move buttons under all that, and 12px of padding above and below. A row is often 150 to 250px high, so a screen shows three or four.

My reading: the web page's Reorder list is what is too tall. The fix keeps every tap target at 44px and removes the rest: a one-line goal, one line of tags, no reason line, less padding, and on a wide screen the moves beside the text.

What follows

  • Every move button and the grip stay 44px, so nothing gets harder to tap.

  • Tags stay on one line and are clipped if there are many. A goal cut short ends in an ellipsis.
