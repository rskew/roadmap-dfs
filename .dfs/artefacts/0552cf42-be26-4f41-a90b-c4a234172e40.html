What would show it wrong

  • You meant "ready" as the existing draft/ready mark, or meant your accept to open the PR by itself.
  • You wanted the PR opened at once, as a GitHub draft PR, with "ready" being GitHub's Ready for review.
  • You wanted the PR's comments answered on GitHub, not only worked in the tree.
