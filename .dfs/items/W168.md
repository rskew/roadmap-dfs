# W168 · in app chat, the scroll-up-to-start-of-message button doesn't work the first time you press it when scrolled to the bottom

## Summary

The chat's ↑ button now scrolls up on the first press when the log is at its end. Before, the keyboard closing (a viewport resize) or a poll redraw pinned the log back to the end and cancelled the smooth scroll; both now wait until the scroll finishes. The Goal is met. I also fixed the chat_top flow's stale 320px nav check, which expected four buttons and failed regardless, and tightened the five nav buttons so "New chat" stays on one line.

## Tree

### W168.1 · A viewport resize or a redraw pins the log to the end and cancels Top's smooth scroll
Status: confirmed
Approach:
- **The question**: why does the chat's ↑ button do nothing the first time it is pressed with the log scrolled to the end?
- **Why it matters**: the chat input is focused when the chat opens. Tapping ↑ moves focus off it, the keyboard closes and the visual viewport resizes. The handler `fit` in `scripts/web/index.html` sees the log still within 40px of its end and sets `scrollTop = scrollHeight`, which cancels the smooth scroll `chatTop` just began. `chatDraw` does the same with its 80px end slack when a poll redraws. Later presses work because the log is no longer at the end.
- **What this node does**: `chatTop` sets a hold that lasts until the log fires `scrollend` (with a timer as fallback). While it is set, neither `fit` nor `chatDraw` pins the log to the end. Add a `chat_top` flow step in `scripts/ui_flows.mjs`: scroll to the end, press ↑ and fire a `visualViewport` resize in the same task, then check the log moved to the last message's top.
- **How we will know it worked**: the new step fails without the fix and passes with it, and `chat_top` and `chat_follow` still pass.
Determination: Confirmed: the step failed without the fix (log stayed at 2992, not 1709) and passes with it, and the chat follow and scroll-memory flows pass (the chat_top flow's last 320px step already failed before this change).
