# W74 · Pin walk controls to top of item view when scrolling

## Summary

On the web page, the Walk box at the head of the task list (Next, Agent, Start or Stop walk, Stop chain) now stays pinned under the top bar while the list scrolls, drawn over the rows, on phones and wide screens alike. Opened, it is capped at about half the screen and scrolls inside itself; reaching its controls with the keyboard leaves the list where it is, while a row, or a grip or button in one, brought into view by the keyboard or a controller lands below it, and a row dragged to reorder scrolls the list when held just under it. The Goal is met on the reading that "the item view" is the list of tasks; a task's own screen, which has only walk tags and a "Walk here next" button, is left as it was, and the page .dfs/artefacts/ddb55a59-ff0b-4721-8a32-86d8e3e890ae.html lets you say if that reading is wrong.

## Goal

Pin walk controls to top of item view when scrolling

Open, it is capped in height and scrolls inside itself, so it never covers the whole list; and the page's top scroll padding grows by its height on the list, so a row brought into view by focus is not left under it. A flow in `scripts/ui_flows.mjs` scrolls a phone's long list to the bottom and checks the box is still on screen, right under the bar, and that rows pass under it.

### W74.3 · Fix: reaching the pinned Walk box from the keyboard scrolls the list away
Parent: W74.1
Status: confirmed
Approach: In `scripts/web/index.html`, `:root:has(#walkbox) { scroll-padding-top: calc(var(--bar-h, var(--bar)) + var(--pin-h, 0px) + var(--s3)); }` reserves the pinned box's own height at the top of the page's snapport, so the box itself (stuck at `--bar-h`) always lies inside that padding, and focusing anything in it makes the browser scroll the page up to "reveal" it. Probed in chromium with the `test_ui.py` fixture (390×600 phone and 1280×500 desktop), list scrolled to the end: focusing `#walkbox > summary` moved `scrollY` 526→283 (phone) and 497→304 (desktop); with the box open, each Tab through its controls scrolled further (749→498 on `#nextsel`, →302 on `#agentsel`, →161 on the next button). So a keyboard or controller user (whose spatial move calls `best.focus(); best.scrollIntoView(...)`) loses their place in the list each time they reach the controls the task pinned to keep in reach. Keep the clearance for rows only (for example a `scroll-margin-top` of `--pin-h` on the rows rather than a root `scroll-padding-top`), so a focused row still lands below the box. To settle it, extend the `walkpin` flow in `scripts/ui_flows.mjs`: scrolled to the end, focus the summary and Tab through the open box, and assert `scrollY` does not change, and keep its check that a focused first row lands below the box.
Determination: Focusing the Walk summary and Tabbing through the open box no longer moves the list, on a 390px phone and a 1280px desktop, and a focused first row still lands below the box: the extended `walkpin` flow fails on the old CSS ("focusing the Walk summary does not scroll the list") and passes 20 runs in a row now, and `test_ui.py` fails only in the five flows that fail on main.

### W74.5 · Fix: a focused grip, move or archive button is left hidden under the pinned Walk box
Parent: W74.3
Status: confirmed
Approach: W74.3 cut the root `scroll-padding-top` in `scripts/web/index.html` to stop at the bar (`:root:has(#walkbox) { scroll-padding-top: calc(var(--bar-h, …) - 1px) }`) and moved the box's clearance onto `.item` alone (`:root:has(#walkbox) .item { scroll-margin-top: … }`). But the list's focusable controls that are not `.item` get no clearance: in reorder mode the rows are `div.item.reorder`, and what takes focus is the `[data-grip]` button and the `[data-move]` ←/→/Break buttons inside them; outside reorder, an archivable task's `button.archive` sits beside its `.item` in `.arow`. scroll-margin is read from the focused element itself, not its row, so the browser treats such a control under the box as already in view. Probed in chromium on the `test_ui.py` fixture (390×600 phone, 1280×500 desktop), Walk box closed and ending at 116px: Shift+Tab up the reorder list from its last grip on the phone left grip W3 focused at 63–107px, wholly under the box; and the controller's `padMove` step (`best.focus(); best.scrollIntoView({ block: "nearest" })`) on a grip or Move-out button lying just under the box left it there (grip at 109, button at 91, box to 116) on both widths. At W74.1 (b8bcbc3) the same probes scrolled each control clear (top 128) and Shift+Tab hid none, so this is a regression of W74.3, and it hides the focused control from the keyboard and controller users the pinned box serves. Give these controls the same clearance as the rows while keeping the box's own controls out of it (for example extend the `scroll-margin-top` rule to `.item :is(button, a, select), .arow > button` or to every focusable in `#view` outside `#walkbox`), and correct the `padMove` comment ("clear of the fixed bars (`scroll-padding`)"), which no longer holds for the list. To settle it, extend `walkpin` in `scripts/ui_flows.mjs`: in reorder mode, scroll so a grip and a Move-out button lie just under the box, focus each as `padMove` does and as Shift+Tab does, and assert each ends below the box's bottom, on the phone and the desktop; it must fail on the current CSS.
Determination: A focused grip, Move button or archive button now comes into view below the pinned Walk box, whether the pad focuses it or Shift+Tab lands on it: the `scroll-margin-top` rule covers `.item`, the buttons and links inside it and `.arow > button`, the `padMove` and `.walk` comments say so, and the extended `walkpin` flow fails on the old CSS (a grip at 96px under a box ending at 116px; Shift+Tab 8 up the reorder list landing at 62px) and passes 3 runs in a row, `test_ui.py` fails only in the five flows that fail on main and `test_web.py` passes; the archive button gets the same rule, but no flow focuses it.
