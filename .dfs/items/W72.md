# W72 · Items can be created in draft state where a walk will skip them (they can be set to next though trigger some work manually)

## Summary

A task can now be created as a draft, from the web form's Draft box, `/api/new` or `run.sh --open`. A draft stays open, but the walk never picks it as the next item, carries on into it or waits on it at a fence; pinning it with `n` or the page's pin, or running `run.sh <task>`, works it like any open task, a usage-limit hold resumes it, and afterwards the walk passes it over again. Marking the item the walk's own chain is on a draft ends that chain after the run in flight, and the walk forecast lists a pinned draft first. The Goal was fully met, and goes a step beyond it: any existing task can be marked a draft or ready again, with `s` in the terminal screen or the toggle on its web page, and the author may want that scope or that key changed. The mark is a `draft`/`ready` entry in the task's own log rather than a marker in `order.md`, since the log travels with the task and records when it changed.

## Goal

Items can be created in draft state where a walk will skip them (they can be set to next though trigger some work manually)

## Tree

### W72.1 · A draft mark in the task's log that the walk's choice skips
Status: confirmed
Approach: `tree.py` gains two log kinds, `draft` and `ready`, and `is_draft(t)` (the later of the two wins; neither means not a draft). `state.py`'s `state_for` carries `draft`; `walk` leaves a draft out of `reachable` for the walk and out of a fence's `pending`; `next_item` and `continue_last` skip it. The status stays `open`, so the screen's `n` pin (`walk_pinned` asks only the item's own status) and `run.sh <task>` still work it. Tests in `test_walk.py`/`test_tree.py`: a draft before an open item is passed over, before a fence it holds nothing, pinned it is taken.
Hypothesis: A draft is a per-task mark in the task's own log, and it only takes the task out of what the walk CHOOSES (the order's next item, the carry-on of the last run's item, and a fence's wait); it does not hold its subtree, and pinning it or running it by hand works it as any open task. Wrong if the author wants a draft to hold the tasks nested under it or a fence after it, or wants the walk to keep working a pinned draft after that chain ends. .dfs/artefacts/db92ef42-b925-4ca5-a430-f012556bceb1.html
Evidence:
- for: `walk.py`'s `walk_target` and `walk_pinned` ask only `status == "open"`, so a draft kept `open` is pinnable with no change there.
- for: `test_order.py` new cases pass (80 OK): a draft before an open task is passed over while the task nested under it is taken, a draft before a fence leaves the fence open, a `ready` after `draft` puts it back in the order, and a draft the last run was on hands the walk back to the order.
Determination: A task whose later `draft`/`ready` log entry is `draft` stays open but is never the walk's next item, carry-on or fence gate, so confirmed.

### W72.7 · Fix: a pinned draft whose chain hits a usage limit is dropped after the hold
Parent: W72.6
Status: confirmed
Approach: `walk.py` `walk_tick` clears the pin (`self.walk_next = None`) when it starts the pinned chain, and after a chain that stopped `limit` it holds and then asks `walk_target()` again, which with no pin is `data["next_item"]`, i.e. `state.py` `continue_last`. `continue_last` now refuses a draft (`not row.get("draft")`), so a draft the author pinned whose chain was cut short by a usage limit is never resumed: after the hold the walk starts a chain on the order's next item and leaves the draft half-done. For any other item `limit` means "wait and try the same thing again" (the WALK_STOP comment in `walk.py`), which is what the author asked for by pinning it. Fix so a hold on a pinned item's chain resumes that item (e.g. keep or restore the pin through a `limit` hold, or have `continue_last` take a draft whose last session ended `limit`), and add a `test_walk.py` `Ticking` case: pin a draft, its chain stops `limit`, after the hold the next chain starts on the draft, not on `next_item`. README's Drafts paragraph ("When that chain ends the walk passes it over again") should then say a usage-limit hold does not end it.
Determination: The walk now remembers the item a pinned chain was started on (`walk_chain_pin`) and restores the pin when that chain stops `limit`, unless a newer pin was set; new `test_walk.py` cases show a pinned draft resumed after the hold, not `next_item`, and a newer pin kept (`test_walk.py` 50 pass; the 12 `test_screen.py` errors are the same before and after this change), and the README Drafts paragraph says a hold does not end the pin, so confirmed.

### W72.8 · Fix: marking the item the walk's chain is on a draft does not take the walk off it
Parent: W72.4
Status: confirmed
Approach: `tui.py` `UI.toggle_draft` (`s`) and `web.py` `act("draft")` (`/api/draft`) only append the `draft` log entry; `run.sh`'s session loop has no draft check, and the walk's chain carries the walk's whole remaining budget as its cap. So when the author marks a draft the item that the walk itself chose and is running a chain on, the chain goes on working it until it is done, blocked or the budget is spent, while the screen says "W5 is a draft: the walk passes it over". `walk_pin` already handles the same situation for a pin by lowering the live chain's cap to the run in flight (`dfs_runs.lower_cap`). Fix so marking a draft the item of the walk's own live chain (`walk_run()`, when it was not pinned by the author) ends that chain after the run in flight, for both the screen and the page, and test it (a `test_walk.py` case asserting `lower_cap` is called for the walked item and not for a pinned one or a chain on another item).
Determination: `WalkMixin.walk_draft_marked` lowers the cap of the walk's own live chain on the item to the run in flight, called from the screen's `s` and the page's `/api/draft` (through `draft_marked` on both walkers), and leaves a pinned chain, a chain on another item and a walk that is off alone; new `test_walk.py` (52) and `test_web.py` cases pass, as do `test_order.py`, `test_webscreen.py` and `test_tree.py`, so confirmed (`test_web.py`'s chat test still fails, as it cannot run its stub from noexec /tmp).

### W72.9 · Fix: the walk forecast shows a draft as held by a fence "until raft is done" and misses a pinned draft as the first item
Parent: W72.4
Status: confirmed
Approach: `tui.py` `UI.lines_plan` sorts items into `take`/`held` by `row_state(it)`, which now returns `draft` for an open draft. A draft therefore lands in `held`, where `why` assumes a state starting `↳` or `»` and prints "a fence, until %s is done" % state[1:], i.e. "a fence, until raft is done". Checked by rendering `lines_plan(80)` with items W7 (open) and W9 (open, draft=1, pinned as `walk_next`): the output lists "1  W7  seven" in the accent as the certain first item, and "W9 nine, a draft — a fence, until raft is done" under "held back", while `walk_target()` is W9. Fix so a pinned draft is listed first in `take`, an unpinned draft is listed with a true reason (e.g. "a draft: the walk passes it over") or under its own heading, and add a `test_order.py`/`test_screen.py` case on `lines_plan` for both.
Determination: `lines_plan` now lists the walk's target first whatever holds it (so a pinned draft is the accent line), and an unpinned draft is held back as "a draft: the walk passes it over, n pins it" rather than as a fence; the new `test_screen.py` case fails without the change and passes with it, `test_order.py`, `test_walk.py` and `test_tree.py` pass, and `test_screen.py` still has its 12 existing curses `initscr` errors, so confirmed.

### W72.10 · Minor findings from the review
Parent: W72.6
Status: confirmed

- README's Drafts paragraph names only the tui's `n` as the pin; the web page's walk pin (`/api/walk/pin`) pins a draft too.
- W72.4 and W72.5 let the author mark an EXISTING task a draft and ready again (`s`, the page's toggle), which the Goal ("created in draft state") does not ask for, and chose `s` as the key, without a Hypothesis saying so; the author should be asked when accepting whether marking existing tasks is wanted and whether `s` is the key they want.
Determination: The screen's draft mark is now logged under the `order` kind, `/api/new` refuses a non-bool `draft` with 400 (new `test_web.py` case), the pinned-draft test says what it guards, and the README names the page's pin; the scope note (marking existing tasks with `s`) is in the Summary for the author, and `test_web.py`, `test_order.py` and `test_walk.py` pass but for the chat test that cannot run its stub from noexec /tmp, so confirmed.
