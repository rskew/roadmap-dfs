# W122 · Sometimes pressing archive on the item opens the item instead

## Summary

Pressing the archive icon now archives in the two cases found to open the item. The icon's touch target is 44px high instead of 32px, so a finger a little above or below the icon no longer lands on the row; and when the list moves between press and release (measured with a real raise written to a task above), the page runs the button the press began on, not the row that has come to lie under the finger. The first cause is far the likelier "sometimes": a list shift needs a row-changing write inside a tap of about a tenth of a second, about 1 in 1,400 taps in this roadmap's busiest hour. The Goal is met for both, but 44px is still under the 9mm one-handed-thumb figure and nothing was measured on a phone, so an off-target press can still open the item; a press whose click lands on no button at all is dropped, and the guard tells buttons apart by act and id only. These limits are written as an assumption for the author to rule on.

## Goal

Sometimes pressing archive on the item opens the item instead
