# W122 · Sometimes pressing archive on the item opens the item instead

## Goal

Sometimes pressing archive on the item opens the item instead

## Tree

### W122.1 · Reproduce in a real browser how the archive button opens the item
Status: confirmed
Approach: Serve the example project with `web.serve` (W4 accepted and pushed so it is archivable), drive headless Chromium over the DevTools pipe at 320, 390, 1280 and 2560 px, and (a) hit-test a 5x5 grid of points over each `.archive` box with `elementFromPoint`, (b) tap it with real touch events, (c) tap it while the list redraws, (d) tap it while a row above changes height between touchstart and touchend, logging pointerdown, pointerup and click targets.
Evidence:
- for: hit-testing the button's box at four widths returned `archive` at all 25 points on both archivable rows, so the button's geometry and stacking are right.
- for: a tap with the list redrawn between touchstart and touchend still opened the archive dialog, so a redraw alone is harmless.
- for: with a row above grown by 30px between touchstart and touchend, pointerdown and pointerup were on `archive:W4` but the click was on `task:W4`, and the item opened; at 60px the click landed on `task:W3`, a different item. At 0 and 12px the dialog opened.
Determination: Confirmed: the click of a touch tap is hit-tested at release, so when the list moves under a finger (a push redraw that changes a row's height) a press that began on the archive button clicks the row beneath it and opens the item; the button's geometry is not at fault.

### W122.2 · A click that lands on a different button than the press began on runs the pressed button
Parent: W122.1
Status: started
Approach: In `scripts/web/index.html` record the `[data-act]` button under each `pointerdown` (act and a copy of its dataset), clear it on `pointercancel` and after each click. In the document click handler, when the click has a pointer (`e.detail > 0`), lands on a `[data-act]` button, and a press was recorded on a different button (different act or id), run the pressed button's act with the recorded dataset instead. Add a `ui_flows.mjs` flow and `test_ui.py` test that press the archive button over CDP touch events, grow a row above by 60px, release, and expect the archive dialog and no change of route.

### W122.3 · Hold list redraws while a pointer is down
Parent: W122.1
Status: parked
Approach: Defer `drawTasks` while a pointer is down on the list and run it on release, as the Walk box already is, so the rows do not move between press and release.
Determination: Parked: it stops only redraw-caused shifts, not others (the list padding `midLayout` adds, late fonts), and a held redraw can leave the list stale; take it if the click-target guard is refuted.
