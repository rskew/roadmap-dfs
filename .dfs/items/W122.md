# W122 · Sometimes pressing archive on the item opens the item instead

## Summary

Pressing the archive icon now archives in the two cases found to open the item. The icon's touch target is 44px high instead of 32px, so a finger a little above or below the icon no longer lands on the row; and when the list moves between press and release (measured with a real raise written to a task above), the page runs the button the press began on, not the row that has come to lie under the finger. The first cause is far the likelier "sometimes": a list shift needs a row-changing write inside a tap of about a tenth of a second, which this roadmap's own history puts at about 1 in 1,400 taps in its busiest hour. The Goal is met for both, but the 44px target is still smaller than the 9mm one-handed-thumb figure and nothing was measured on a phone, so an off-target press can still open the item; a press whose click lands on no button at all is still dropped.
