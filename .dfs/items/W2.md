## Summary

Finished tasks now recede in the task list on both the terminal screen and the phone page: their row is dim or muted and lighter in weight, and their status reads `✓ done`, while open and blocked tasks stay at full weight. The Goal was read as the list of tasks, not the nodes inside a task's tree, which already mark confirmed ones with a coloured rule on the web page, so the terminal tree was not touched.
