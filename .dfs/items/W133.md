## Summary

The Refute/confirm form now scrolls as a single panel inside the space a phone keyboard leaves, with Cancel and Save always in view, and a text box you are typing in scrolls clear of those buttons. The same change applies to every other form that opens in that dialog.

## Goal

## Tree

### W133.1 · Make the form dialog one scroller that keeps the focused field clear of the sticky buttons
Status: confirmed
Approach: In scripts/web/index.html, the `#form` dialog (the Refute/confirm form) is capped at the visual viewport height (`--vvh`), but its inner `form` is also capped at the full `--vvh`, so the form overflows the dialog's border by 2px and the dialog scrolls as well as the form (two nested scrollers; measured in headless chromium with `--vvh` 200-420px). Fix in CSS: `#form` gets `overflow: hidden`, the form's max-height subtracts the dialog's border, and the form gets `scroll-padding-bottom` for the sticky Cancel/Save row so a focused textarea is not left under it. In the `visualViewport` fit handler, scroll the focused field of `#form` into view (`block: "nearest"`) after the keyboard moves the viewport. Check with a static harness page of the real design.css and page CSS at `--vvh` 200, 300 and 420, with and without the hypothesis iframe: one scroller, Save fully visible, textarea reachable.
Determination: Confirmed in headless chromium at `--vvh` 200, 300 and 420 with and without the iframe: the dialog no longer scrolls, the form is the one scroller, Save stays visible, and a focused textarea scrolls to clear the buttons; a real phone keyboard was not run, and test_web's two agent/login tests fail for reasons unrelated to the page.
