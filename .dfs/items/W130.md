## Summary

A change is now judged on two tiers: the delivered tree is scored against a rubric of the task's Goal clauses by a blind kiro judge that ties within 1.5 clauses (`docs/dfs-rubrics/`, `docs/dfs-quality-judge.py`), and where quality ties by credits (`docs/dfs-quality-pairs.py`, step 5 of `docs/dfs-optimization.md`); a change that does nothing is kept 2.8% of the time, and only one pair of control trees (in W11) differs in quality, so credits mostly decide. The rubric's reading of the three-word Goal "Render rich text" (lists and fences included) is an assumption, stated with a page for the author; the verdict holds on either reading. Left out: the judge's cost is unknown (kiro-cli prints no credits for it), W22's changed replay was not run again because no cost it could have would make that pair better, shop-app and farm-app still cannot be replayed, and the installed nix build of `replay.py` is older than the repo's.
