- Runs a control and a changed arm in one sitting under kiro and judges the pair by step 5 of `docs/dfs-optimization.md` (rubric quality, then credits). If credits per replay make a pair unaffordable, says the cost and bounds it from the history instead (`docs/dfs-fix-regressions.tsv`: 14 of 37 later rounds are made of fix-caused or fix-left lines alone).

**How we will know it worked**
- One scored pair, or a stated cost that stops it, and `docs/dfs-optimization.md` row 3 and the sixth candidate say what the dearer tasks show.
