## Summary

The optimisation loop was run once from this box. The history of all three checkouts was recounted, twenty kiro replays of four roadmap-dfs tasks were run, and the replay tooling was fixed where it failed. A change is now judged on the quality of the delivered tree against a rubric of the task's Goal clauses, scored by a blind kiro judge that ties within 1.5 clauses, and where quality ties by credits (`docs/dfs-quality-pairs.py`, step 5 of `docs/dfs-optimization.md`); a change that does nothing is kept 2.8% of the time. Six changes were weighed (candidate 2, naming assumptions, was not run) and none is kept:
- a closing "break it" node: better in one pair (W5, lists and fenced code, a reading of "Render rich text" the author may dispute), the same in W36, unresolved in W22;

- a SKILL.md without its unenforced lines: tied on quality, 1.5 times the credits on W36;
- stopping refusals at the source: about 3% of spend at most;
- a cap of three issues reviews: 11.9% of units fall after the third, but 11 of 23 finding lines in the rounds it cuts are defects and a raise stops the task for the author, so it rests on the author's preference.

No candidate was merged into SKILL.md or any text a session reads. The tooling fixes (`history.py`'s verdict reading, `replay.py`'s `--recorded-as`, `--limit`, `--shell`, `--also`) are code changes with tests, made inside this task though the roadmap says tooling faults are raised; the author may say where they belong.
