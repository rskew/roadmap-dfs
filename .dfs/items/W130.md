# W130 · Optimise

## Summary

The optimisation loop was run once from this box. The history of all three checkouts was recounted (`docs/dfs-class-counts.py`), twenty kiro replays of four roadmap-dfs tasks were run, and the replay tooling was fixed where it failed (a review verdict written through a shell variable, a read-only docs tree on resume, `--recorded-as` for a checkout recorded under another path, `--limit` refused where unused). A change is now judged on the quality of the delivered tree against a rubric of the task's Goal clauses, scored by a blind kiro judge that ties within 1.5 clauses (`docs/dfs-rubrics/`, `docs/dfs-quality-judge.py`), and where quality ties by credits (`docs/dfs-quality-pairs.py`, step 5 of `docs/dfs-optimization.md`); a change that does nothing is kept 2.8% of the time. The one candidate tried, a closing "break it" node, is better in one pair (W5, on lists and fenced code, a reading of "Render rich text" the rubric makes and the author may dispute), the same in W36 and unresolved in W22, so it is not kept and stays unmerged.

The Goal is met for roadmap-dfs and only partly for the optimisation-data projects: shop-app and farm-app were mined but not replayed. A shop-app task prepares, and with `replay.py prepare --shell shop-app-e2e` its replay sandbox now holds nix, node, playwright and a working chromium (a spec runs up to its global setup), but the e2e check also needs the database and the backend running inside it, which is not done, so a work session there still cannot run the specs; no farm-app task qualifies by the loop's rule. Also left out: the judge's cost is unknown and its blindness rests on its prompt, the script that pairs trees has no test, and the installed nix build of `replay.py` is older than the repo's.
