### W130.8 · Choose a keep rule the control's 9 of 12 allows, and say why
Parent: W130.4
Status: confirmed
Approach: The control passes the first review in 9 of 12 replays, so "four more passes of twelve" cannot be met. In docs/dfs-optimization.md (the keep rule, near line 464) and W130.6, replace it with a rule a change can meet and a no-op cannot: read the 12 control outcomes (pass or issues per task and round, credits per replay, credits spent after a review said issues) against the options (a) restrict the comparison to W5 and W36, where the control fails 3 of 6, and require the change to pass all of those and no task worse; (b) compare credits per replay, with the control's spread (W5 12.2 to 23.4) as the margin; (c) more replays per arm. Write the chosen rule as a Hypothesis with its artefact page, edit the document, then W130.6 follows it.
Hypothesis: A change that works passes the first review in every one of its 12 replays, and a no-op does so rarely enough (3.2% if the control's true pass rate is 0.75, at most 25% anywhere in the 9-of-12 interval 0.43 to 0.95 once the same-sitting control must not also pass 12 of 12), so "all 12 pass, the same-sitting control at most 11, 12 replays costing at most 176 credits" is a rule the control allows and a no-op rarely meets. Wrong if the control's true pass rate is above 0.85 (a no-op then passes 12 of 12 in 14% or more) or if the first review's verdict depends on the replay's sitting more than on the tool. .dfs/artefacts/1b9b28c6-5f6a-49cc-b988-4638ffc650c5.html
Evidence:
- for: the 12 control logs (W22 ok ok ok, W11 ok ok ok, W36 ok issues ok, W5 issues issues ok) give 9 of 12, so +4 is 13 of 12 and the old rule keeps nothing; all 12 is +3 and a binomial sum gives a no-op 3.2% at 0.75 and 1.4% at 0.70.
- against: 9 of 12 has a 95% interval of 0.43 to 0.95 (bisection), so the no-op rate is unbounded above except by the same-sitting control condition (at most 25%); and a change that lifts p to 0.95 is kept only 54% of the time.
- against: the credit margin of 176 is the control total 155.0 plus two spreads of a difference (10.4) from only 3 replays a task, W5 alone ranges 12.2 to 23.4.
Determination: Chose "all 12 first reviews pass, the same-sitting control at most 11 of 12, the 12 replays at most 176 credits" and wrote it into docs/dfs-optimization.md step 5 with the reasons the three options were rejected (W5 and W36 only regress to the mean, credits alone sit below the control's spread, five replays a task cost about 340 credits an arm); the page's layout was read from the mobile screenshot only, because this box renders no text in the checker (its blank check fails on a page from an earlier task too).
