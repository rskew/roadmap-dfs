# W130 · Optimise

## Summary

The optimisation loop was run once from this box. The history of all three checkouts was recounted, twenty kiro replays of four roadmap-dfs tasks were run, and the replay tooling was fixed where it failed. A change is now judged on the quality of the delivered tree against a rubric of the task's Goal clauses, scored by a blind kiro judge that ties within 1.5 clauses, and where quality ties by credits (`docs/dfs-quality-pairs.py`, step 5 of `docs/dfs-optimization.md`); a change that does nothing is kept 2.8% of the time. Six changes were weighed (candidate 2, naming assumptions, was not run) and none is kept:
- a closing "break it" node: better in one pair (W5, lists and fenced code, a reading of "Render rich text" the author may dispute), the same in W36, unresolved in W22;
- a more thorough first review and re-running checks after a fix: the replays show one second round in sixteen, but they are cheap tasks, which hold 0.6% of the spend after a second issues review against 99% on the dearer ones, so that bound does not carry to the loop; a review told to walk every clause named 6 of 12 unmet clauses against 3, at 1.49 times the credits; one pair on W17, a task above $3.5, read better (8.5 of 10 clauses against 5.0 and 6.5) but removed no round and cost 18 sessions and at least 94.7 credits against 4 and 20 to 24, unmerged at the cap; one pair is not a keep, and whether wording or extra rounds made the quality is untested; its rubric was written after reading the original run's findings and two clauses, 2 of the 3.5-clause lead, are cases those later reviews found, which favours a longer loop;
- a SKILL.md without its unenforced lines: tied on quality, 1.5 times the credits on W36;
- stopping refusals at the source: about 3% of spend at most;
- a cap of three issues reviews: 11.9% of units fall after the third, but 11 of 23 finding lines in the rounds it cuts are defects and a raise stops the task for the author, so it rests on the author's preference.

No candidate was merged into SKILL.md or any text a session reads. The tooling fixes (`history.py`'s verdict reading, which no longer counts a review command that failed or that `tree.py` refused, `replay.py`'s `--recorded-as`, `--limit`, `--shell`, `--also`) are code changes with tests, made inside this task though the roadmap says tooling faults are raised; the author may say where they belong.

The Goal is met for roadmap-dfs and partly for the optimisation-data projects. shop-app and farm-app were mined. shop-app's sandbox holds nix, node, playwright, chromium, Postgres and the backend (`pricing-detail.spec.js` passes 25 of 25 there; a backend rebuild after an edit was not tried) and one control replay of W32 ran (first review ok, 25.9 credits, 7 and 8 of 9 clauses). farm-app's sessions are recorded under `/workspace/chirpstack-docker`; of 8 candidate tasks W132 was replayed once as a control (first review issues, 10 sessions and 62.4 credits to the cap, 11 of 12 clauses). Neither ranks a candidate. Also left out: the judge's cost, and credit for findings no rubric clause covers.
