# W39 · Remove Ask, keep only Hypothesis

## Goal

A node records a choice the author may want made differently in one way only: the Hypothesis, with its "Wrong if", its Evidence and its artefact. Sessions can no longer write `Ask:`; the docs, the checker, the screens and the tests say so. Existing Asks in committed items stay as they are and still display and count, and no Hypothesis, existing or new, is weakened or lost.

Why: Asks are about as common as Hypotheses (37 against 32 in the items now), and most are light on reasoning. An Ask has no "Wrong if" and no evidence, so nothing can refute it, and it lets a session skip declaring what it assumed.
