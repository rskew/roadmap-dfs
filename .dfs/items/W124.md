### W124.3 · find why opencode's turn sometimes reports the model's socket closed
Parent: W124.2
Status: confirmed
Approach: In about 3 of 8 runs of test_opencode_chat.py a page turn (`run --standalone --format json --session`) ends in an `error` event, "Transport: The socket connection was closed unexpectedly", right after the fake model (test_agent_models.FakeOpenAIModel, an HTTP/1.0 server that streams SSE and closes) was asked. Decide whether the fake server's way of ending the stream (no length, no chunking, close right after `[DONE]`) is what Bun's fetch rejects, by running the same `opencode run --standalone` turn against the model 30 times with and without HTTP/1.1 chunked replies and counting errors; fix whichever side it is (the fake model, or a retry in chat.py if a real endpoint can do it too), then rerun test_opencode_chat.py 8 times.
Determination: The "socket closed" / "Unable to connect" errors were opencode 2.1.1's own private server (`serve --stdio`, started by `run --standalone`) crashing in Bun 1.4.2 with a Bus error 0.3s after start (seen in `--print-logs` stderr of a failing run, never in 30 plain runs against the same fake model), so the HTTP/1.0 fake model was not the cause; run.sh's new opencode_turn runs a turn again, twice at most, only when the stream holds a `Transport:` error and no step_start, and test_chat.py covers it with a stub (59 tests pass). Live test_opencode_chat.py still failed 4 of 8 runs after that, with no crash and no retry in those runs, from a different fault.
