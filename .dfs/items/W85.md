## Goal

Raise artefacts shouldn't follow a strict template, they should be free to express their issue in the best way, focusing on elucidating the key mechanisms and implications at different options, as well as showing the mechanisms of relevant parts of the system. Emphasis on showing visually. Raise artefacts should have a form that submits the answer though.
