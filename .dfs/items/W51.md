## Tree

### W51.1 · Keep the list's scroll anchored when Reorder is switched on or off
Status: confirmed
Approach: In scripts/web/index.html, the `reorder` handler redraws the whole list (drawTasks sets innerHTML), and reorder rows are taller (a strip of five move buttons under each), so the same scrollY lands on different content. Before the redraw, record the first row whose bottom is below the viewport top (an `.item`, keyed by data-id or data-row) and its offset from the viewport top; after the redraw, scroll by the difference so that row sits where it did. Do this in `showList`, which both the reorder toggle and `only` (which leaves reorder mode) go through. Add a ui_flows.mjs flow that scrolls a list long enough to scroll on a phone, toggles Reorder on and off, and checks the anchor row's top stays put (red before the change, green after).
Determination: Confirmed: the new flow reorderkeepsscroll went red before the change (top row W3 became W7 on switching Reorder on) and passes after, on and off; ![the list in Reorder, scrolled to W8 and W9, after the toggle](.dfs/artefacts/1bb52fed-3f82-435a-bdd3-1f18a28bd95f.png) The Goal is met. The existing reorder flow fails the same way (a 409 console error from its refused move) with and without the change.
