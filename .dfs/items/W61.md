# W61 · Chat: add button to scroll to top of current message

## Summary

The chat now has a ↑ Top button in its bottom bar. It scrolls the conversation to the top of the message you are reading: the latest message when you are at the end of the chat, otherwise the one at the top of the view, and each further press steps back to the nearest message above. It holds while the agent is working, because the log is no longer pulled back to the bottom by each "Thinking…" refresh unless you were already at the end or have just sent a message, and it keeps moving up from the end even when the last few messages are short. "Current message" was read as the one in view, since the Goal does not say; the change is confined to the web page and covered by a browser test.

## Goal

Chat: add button to scroll to top of current message

## Tree

### W61.1 · A Top button in the chat that scrolls to the top of the message being read
Status: confirmed
Approach: In scripts/web/index.html add a fourth button, "↑ Top" (`data-act=chattop`), to the chat's bottom nav. `chatTop()` scrolls `#chat-log` to the top of the current message: the last message when the log is at its end, otherwise the message crossing the log's top edge; when already at that message's top it steps to the one before. Add a `chat_top` flow to scripts/ui_flows.mjs and test_ui.py that opens a long chat, taps Top and checks the scroll lands on a message's top.
Determination: The chat's bottom nav now has a ↑ Top button that scrolls to the top of the message being read; the new chat_top flow passes, and the three chat flows that fail (chat, chats section, bar from chats) fail the same way without the change. ![the chat opened at the top of a long answer with the Top button in the nav](.dfs/artefacts/70f19b73-205b-4b78-9ba5-78a4794ca00f.png)

### W61.2 · Top holds while the agent is busy
Parent: W61.1
Status: confirmed
Approach: In scripts/web/index.html `chatDraw` ends with `else if (atEnd || st.busy) log.scrollTop = log.scrollHeight`, and the "Thinking… Ns" text changes the signature every poll, so during a turn the log is snapped to the bottom about every 1.5 s and a Top press is undone. Follow the end only when the reader was at the end (`atEnd`); on Send, scroll to the end explicitly so the new question and the Thinking line show. Extend the `chat_top` flow in scripts/ui_flows.mjs with a busy state whose `busy_for` ticks between polls, and check the log stays at the message top after Top.
Determination: The chat now follows the end of the log only for a reader already at the end or one who has just sent, so Top holds through a turn's "Thinking… Ns" redraws; the extended chat_top flow fails without the change (3210 != 74) and passes with it, and the chat, chats section and bar from chats flows fail as they did before. No picture: the change is a scroll position that holds, which a still frame cannot show.

### W61.3 · Fix: Top does nothing at the end of a chat whose last messages are shorter than the view
Parent: W61.1
Status: confirmed
Approach: In scripts/web/index.html `chatTop`, a log at its end starts from the last non-wait message and steps back once (`at - tops[i] < 4`, which is also true when the top lies below the scroll position). When the last two or more messages are each shorter than the log's visible height (short answers after a long one), every top in that tail lies below the current scrollTop, so `scrollTo` is clamped to the max scroll, which is where the log already is, and the press does nothing; the next press does the same. Replace the single step back with `while (tops[i] > at - 4 && i > 0) i--`, so Top always lands on the nearest message that starts above the view. Extend the `chat_top` flow (taller 520px view) with a Thinking line under a short question and then three short messages, checking each press lands on the nearest message above the view.
Determination: Top now always moves up from the end of a chat, to the nearest message that starts above the view; the extended chat_top flow passes with the change and fails without it (3254 != 1709), and the chat, chats section and bar from chats flows fail as they did before. No picture: the change is a scroll position, which a still frame cannot show.
