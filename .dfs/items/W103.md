# W103 · Add parameter to breaks that stops walks, enabling the user to review before continuing work by starting another walk

## Summary

A `--- stop` line in `order.md` is now a stop break: a walk that had to wait at it ends once everything above is done, so the author can review and start another walk, which passes it. It can be set and shown from the terminal (`B`), the web page (a Stop break button beside Break, drawn with a double rule) and `order.md` itself, and the README says how it works. The Goal was met as written; a pinned task goes past a stop break and a walk started after the review passes it, which is how "starting another walk" was read.

## Goal

Add parameter to breaks that stops walks, enabling the user to review before continuing work by starting another walk

### W103.2 · The walk turns off at a stop break it had to wait for
Parent: W103.1
Status: confirmed
Approach: `walk.py`: each tick, a walk records every stop break that is holding something (an item behind it with `waiting_on` set); it takes no task at or after the first such break except one the author pinned, and once its chains have ended with that break open (everything before it done) it turns itself off saying it stopped at the break, so a walk the author starts next passes it. Tests in `test_walker.py`, through `dfs_state.walk` so the fence is the derived one.
Hypothesis: A stop break ends only the walk that had to wait at it (found something before it unfinished while it ran), once everything before it is done and that walk's chains have ended; a walk started after it opened passes it, and a pinned task goes past it. Wrong if the author means it to end every walk that reaches it, even one started after the review, or to end the walk the moment the fence opens without waiting for the chains, or to hold a pinned task too. .dfs/artefacts/d46247b7-41c0-46a0-8bf2-eb160078003c.html
Evidence:
- for: the Goal says the user reviews and then continues "by starting another walk", which a stop break that ended every walk at it would refuse until the break was taken away.
- for: `test_walker.py`'s new `StopBreaks` tests pass; with `walk_stop_at` forced to return None, the two that check the stop (the walk ends there; nothing shows as next at the break) fail and the four guards (plain break goes on, a later walk passes, a raise waits, a pin goes past) still pass.
Determination: A walk ends at a stop break it waited at, once everything before it is done and its chains have ended, and the next walk passes it; `test_walker.py` (21) and `test_walk.py` pass.

### W103.6 · Fix: a walk whose slots were all taken when it started must still record the stop break it waits at
Parent: W103.2
Status: confirmed
Approach: `walk.py` `walk_tick` calls `walk_stop_at()`, the only call that records a held stop break into `walk_stops`, after the early returns for `free < 1` and `time.time() < self.walk_until`. So a walk whose every slot is filled by a chain it did not start never records the fence while it waits, and goes past it. Checked with `FakeWalker` from `test_walker.py`: W1 open in segment 0, W2 behind a `stop` fence in segment 1, a live hand-started chain on W1 (`run_dirs=[run("h1","W1",live=True)]`), `start(10)` leaves `walk_stops` empty; after W1 is done and that chain has ended, the next tick starts a chain on W2 and the walk stays on. That breaks the Goal: the author starts a chain on the last task before the review by hand, starts a walk to carry on after it, and the walk works past the point where they meant to review. Record what is held on every tick while the walk is on (for example, call `walk_stop_at()` before those returns, or record in a step of its own), and add a `StopBreaks` test for this case that fails before the fix.
Determination: `walk_tick` now records the held stop break on every tick the walk is on, before its hold and no-free-slot returns, so a walk started beside a hand-started chain on the last task before the break ends there; the new `StopBreaks` test fails before the change (the walk starts W2) and passes after, with `test_walker` and `test_walk` (103) passing.
