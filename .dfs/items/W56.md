# W56 · Reorder mode controls

## Summary

In the web page's Reorder mode, each row now has a left arrow (move out of its parent) and a right arrow (move under the task above) in place of the Out and In buttons, and a grip at its right edge in place of Up and Down. Dragging the grip lifts the row with its subtree, shows a line where it will land, and writes the new position to the order file; the focused grip also moves on the up and down arrow keys. A drag keeps the task under its own parent, as Up and Down did; changing the parent stays with the arrows. It was exercised with a mouse in a phone-sized browser and not with a real touch screen.

## Goal

Replace in out with left right arrows, replace up down with grab handle on the right allowing dragging item into place
