### W135.3 · Enter sends on a desktop keyboard
Parent: W135.2
Status: open
Approach: **The question**
- Can Enter send the message, with Shift+Enter for a new line, without breaking phone typing?

**What this node does**
- In the `#chat-input` keydown handler in `scripts/web/index.html`, Enter submits when the device has a fine pointer (`matchMedia("(pointer: fine)")`); on a touch screen Enter stays a new line. Ctrl/cmd+Enter keeps sending everywhere.
- Changes the placeholder to say which key sends.

**How we will know it worked**
- A headless check at 1200px: Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a line; at a touch 360px viewport Enter adds a line. Screenshot of the placeholder.

### W135.4 · Keep the unsent draft per chat
Parent: W135.3
Status: open
Approach: **The question**
- Can a half-typed message survive closing and reopening the dialog?

**What this node does**
- Stores `#chat-input` text in `sessionStorage` under the chat's scope on input, restores it in `chatOpen` (which now stops clearing it), and removes it when the message is sent or "New chat" is pressed.

**How we will know it worked**
- Type, press Escape, reopen: the text is back. Send: the field is empty on reopen.

### W135.5 · A way back to the newest message
Parent: W135.4
Status: open
Approach: **The question**
- How does a reader who scrolled up get back to the newest message in one tap?

**What this node does**
- Shows a small "Newest ↓" button over the bottom of `#chat-log` while the log is more than about 80px from its end, using the existing scroll listener; it scrolls to the end and hides.

**How we will know it worked**
- Scroll up in a long chat: the button shows at 360px; tap it: the log is at its end and the button is gone. Screenshot.
