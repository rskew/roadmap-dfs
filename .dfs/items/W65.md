## Summary

This instance's web page now has its own background: the app icon's drawing (the stepped path, amber node, red stopped branch and blue end) repeated across the page, one picture for light mode and one for dark, under the page's usual veil so text stays readable. It needed no code, only the two pictures in `.dfs/` and the script that draws them (`.dfs/background-src`). I read "extension of icon theme" as carrying the icon's drawing and colours into the background; if the app icons themselves were meant to follow the project's colour, or only a flat colour was wanted, those readings are set aside as alternatives. The terminal screen is unchanged, and the page was seen in a headless browser, not on a phone.
