  The `pad` flow is flaky under load, and W60.15's "five runs in a row" does not hold then. With other test runs on the machine (load average 17) it failed 5 of 6 runs at "down moves focus lower (4 to 0)" or "(4 to 4)"; the base commit 2e4de0c failed 2 of 6, and on a quiet machine every run passed. One run showed the second down press leaving focus on the rename button, so a draw undid the move. Likely cause: `render()` in `scripts/web/index.html` reads `focusKey(document.activeElement)` before `await renderDraw(force)` and refocuses that stale key after it when the draw dropped focus. A d-pad move made while a fetch is in flight is undone, or lost to the body when the key was null because the pad was not yet on. A server push during a d-pad press can do the same for a real user. The fix is to read the key after the fetch, just before the draw, and to skip the restore if focus moved meanwhile. I did not confirm this cause.
