# W46 · It's unclear from the indentation when a node is a sibling or a child

## Summary

The task tree, on the web page and in the terminal, now steps every child in: a fork's branches a full step and a chain step half a step, so a node is never level with its parent and siblings always share a column. A long chain stays narrow because the half steps stop growing after eight steps. The Goal is met on both surfaces; the earlier rule that put a chain at its parent's column was what made a child look like a sibling. A full step for every child and a connector line between rows were left out: the first brings back a long staircase, the second does not change the indentation the Goal is about.

## Goal

It's unclear from the indentation when a node is a sibling or a child
