# W49 · When there a no items left, a walk should pend, and process new items when they are created

## Summary

A walk with no workable task left no longer turns itself off: it stays on, shows "waiting for a task" in the screen's header and on the page, spends no budget, and starts a chain as soon as a task becomes workable (a new task file, or a raise answered). This holds both after a chain ends with nothing left and when the walk is started with nothing to take. The Goal was fully met; the walk still ends when its budget is spent, on a failure, or when stopped by hand. Not run against a real agent: the behaviour is covered by the walk's tick tests and the headless walker's test.
