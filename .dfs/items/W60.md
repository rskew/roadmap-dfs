# W60 · Big screen experience

## Goal

Propose a few different ways we could enable a good experience with a big screen. This might be via plugging the mobile into a dock, or either way maybe interacted with via a game controller. Go broad!

## Tree

### W60.1 · Survey what the page and the terminal do on a big screen today
Status: confirmed
Approach: Read `scripts/web/index.html` and `design.css` for the wide-screen layout (breakpoints, `--page`, `--col`, type and hit sizes) and for every keyboard and pointer input, and read the TUI's `KEYS` table; serve `scripts/web.py` on a spare port and screenshot the task page at 1920x1080 and at a 10-foot TV size (1920x1080 at 2x zoom, i.e. 960x540 CSS px). Record what is wasted, what is unreachable without a mouse and what already helps, as the ground the proposals start from.
Determination: Nothing on a big screen is built: at 1920 and 3840 px wide the page is capped at 1180 px (`--page`, a two-pane list and sheet from 900 px), body type stays 17 px and buttons stay 44 px at any size, the only keys are `j` `k` `n` Esc plus arrow keys on a reorder grip, the TUI has its own `KEYS` table, and nothing reads the Gamepad API. ![The task page on a 3840x2160 screen: content in the middle 30%](.dfs/artefacts/2c29d392-ad43-40b2-af1c-944523ff2c9a.png)
