# W166 · Update the icon to be similar to the background

## Summary

The app icon is now cut from the real background tree: same branches, line weights, colours and marks, shown as a round disc around the trunk and the middle of the crown, with transparent corners. It is drawn by the same script as the background pictures, so the two stay alike, and the background pictures are unchanged. The Goal is met on one reading of "similar": the icon is the background seen closer, in the light colours only. A shrunk whole background and a denser redrawn tree were left out because the whole picture turns to fuzz at small sizes and a redraw is what looked unlike the background.

## Goal

Update the icon to be similar to the background

## Tree

### W166.1 · Cut the app icon from the real background tree
Status: confirmed
Approach: **The question**
- What should "an icon similar to the background" be? Today's `.dfs/icon` is a sparse redrawn tree that does not look like the background.

**What this node does**
- In `.dfs/background-src/make_background.py`, change `icon()` to draw the background's own tree (same seed and settings as the light background) and show only a square around its trunk and crown (viewBox 220 300 1240 1240 of the 1680 picture), scaled to 1024.
- Render it with `render.sh` to `.dfs/icon` and drop the now-unused icon-only tree settings.

**How we will know it worked**
- The rendered icon has the background's branches, line weights and marks, and the trunk and forks still read at 60 px. Serve the project and check `/icon-192.png` and `/manifest.json` give the new picture.
Hypothesis: "Similar to the background" means the icon is the real background tree, cut square around its trunk and crown, in the light colours. Wrong if the author wants the whole background shrunk, a denser redrawn tree, or a dark icon. .dfs/artefacts/ba6184b3-79c7-4565-be8d-5cf372d2a4dd.html
Evidence:
- for: re-running `make_background.py` and `render.sh` after the edit gave background pngs that match `.dfs/background-image-light` and `-dark` byte for byte, so the background is untouched and the icon is cut from the same drawing.
- for: the icon at 120, 60 and 32 px (on the answer page) keeps the thick trunk, the amber forks and the red stops; only the finest twigs blur.
- against: the crown is cut at the top and sides, so the icon shows part of the tree, not all of it.

### W166.3 · It's for an app icon so make it round?
Parent: W166
Status: confirmed
Approach: **The question**
- The author asks whether the icon should be round, since it is an app icon. Should `.dfs/icon` be a disc?

**What this node does**
- In `.dfs/background-src/make_background.py`, `icon()` clips the same cut of the background tree to the circle inside its square (a `clipPath` disc, radius half the side), so the corners are empty.
- In `.dfs/background-src/render.mjs`, the screenshot is taken with `omitBackground: true`, so those corners stay transparent in the png. The background pictures have an opaque ground, so they do not change.
- Re-render `.dfs/icon`.

**How we will know it worked**
- The icon png is RGBA with alpha 0 in all four corners, and the light and dark background pictures are byte for byte as before.
Hypothesis: "Make it round" means the icon picture itself is a disc with transparent corners, in the same tree cut as before. Wrong if the author wants the square kept and left to the phone to mask, with the art held inside a safe zone. .dfs/artefacts/7d2f9c41-8a6e-4b35-9e07-1c4a5b8d3f60.html
Evidence:
- for: decoding the rendered png, the four corners are (0,0,0,0) and the centre is opaque; the file is 1024 x 1024 RGBA.
- for: re-rendering the light and dark backgrounds gives files identical (`cmp`) to `.dfs/background-image-light` and `-dark`.
- against: the icon is served as purpose "any" only; on iOS a transparent corner is painted black, so the home-screen tile there is a black square with a disc on it. I did not run it on a device.
- against: the disc loses the four corners of the square cut, so a little more of the crown is cut off than before.
![the round icon, 1024px](.dfs/artefacts/3e9a4d52-7b1c-4f68-a0d3-5c2e8b7f1a94.png)
Determination: Confirmed: `.dfs/icon` is now a disc with transparent corners cut from the background tree; the icon web test passes, and the other web test failures are README checks that do not touch the icon.
