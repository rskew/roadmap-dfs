Design system

Principles

Calm. Quiet paper, hairline rules, the system's own type. Three colours, kept for meaning, never for decoration. Square corners, no shadows, no icons but the theme switch and the dfs mark. The one flourish is the line of the three under the bar.

Colour

Surfaces and text, then the three meanings. Each meaning has an ink (text), a wash (a tint for a whole row or card) and, for amber, a fill. The ratio is measured live, text on its own surface, in the theme you are looking at; the floor is 4.5 to 1.
